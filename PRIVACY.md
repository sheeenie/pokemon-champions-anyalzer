# PikaLens Privacy Policy

_Effective 28 September 2026_

PikaLens is a Mac app that reads the battle on a phone connected to your Mac
and shows information about it. It does not collect, store or share any
personal data.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your phone's screen.** PikaLens reads the screen of the iPhone or Android
  phone you connect over USB and analyses it on your Mac. Screen images are
  never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere.
- **Camera access.** macOS provides a connected iPhone's screen to apps as a
  camera, so PikaLens asks for camera permission. It uses that permission only
  to read the connected phone's screen, not your Mac's camera.
- **Android connection.** To connect to an Android phone, PikaLens creates a USB
  debugging key and keeps it on your Mac. It is used only to talk to your own
  phone.
- **Settings and cached data.** Your language choice and a cache of move-usage
  statistics are stored on your Mac, inside PikaLens's own app container.

## What PikaLens sends over the internet

PikaLens requests publicly available move-usage statistics from
[championsbattledata.com](https://championsbattledata.com). Each request
contains only the identifier of the battler being looked up, nothing about you.
As with any web request, that site receives your IP address; its handling of
requests is governed by its own policies.

PikaLens sends nothing else. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising,
tracking or crash reporting of its own.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's
settings, Apple may pass crash reports to the developer. That is controlled by
Apple and by you, not by PikaLens.

## Children

PikaLens collects no data from anyone, including children.
